# Upstream provenance

`src/hashline/`, `UPSTREAM_README.md`, and `UPSTREAM_CHANGELOG.md` were copied
from `packages/hashline` in Oh My Pi commit
`644ad30d6e9436074a00f8bd08ecadcd98992fc1` by Can Bölük.

and the Pi extension adapter in `src/index.ts` are local adaptations. The copied
implementation retains its full MIT notice in `LICENSE`; package distribution
also includes `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`.
