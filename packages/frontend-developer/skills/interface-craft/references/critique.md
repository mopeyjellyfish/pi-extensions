# Critique

Modified from Impeccable 4.1.1 at `56f44523f76efdcec813e67b38ee550e49b16f48` under Apache-2.0.

## Scope
Evaluate hierarchy, clarity, craft, and task fit without changing the product.

## Evidence
Cite observed screens, states, and design authority; separate findings from hypotheses.

## Handoff
Offer a follow-on to `implement` or `developing-changes` only when requested.

## Completion
Ranked findings and unmet proof are reported; critique remains evaluation-only.
