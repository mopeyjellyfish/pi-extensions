# Distill

Modified from Impeccable 4.1.1 at `56f44523f76efdcec813e67b38ee550e49b16f48` under Apache-2.0.

## Scope

Simplify an interface to the minimum information, controls, and decisions needed for its primary task.

## Diagnose

Map the task path, decision points, repeated explanations, redundant controls, premature options, container clutter, and secondary content competing with the focal action.

## Evidence

Show what is removed, combined, deferred, or renamed and prove the task still works for first use, repeat use, empty, error, permission, and long-content states.

## Guardrails

Do not hide required context, destructive consequences, recovery, accessibility labels, expert efficiency, or product capability merely to reduce element count.

## Handoff

Separate reversible presentation cuts from behavioral or information-architecture changes. Delegate the latter to `implement` or `developing-changes` when available.

## Completion

The interface has fewer decisions and distractions while remaining complete, discoverable, accessible, and faster to understand.
