# Design

Modified from Impeccable 4.1.1 at `56f44523f76efdcec813e67b38ee550e49b16f48` under Apache-2.0.

## Scope

Establish or materially revise a deliberate interface direction for a web product surface.

## Diagnose

Resolve the person, task, surface mode, incumbent authority, focal workflow, desired feel, representative states, and responsive constraints. Name generic defaults the product must reject.

## Evidence

For material direction, compare two to eight inspected image-backed directions and record the explicit choice. Separate visible facts, accepted decisions, hypotheses, and unavailable proof.

## Guardrails

Preserve product truth, accessible native controls, and the target framework. Do not treat a missing `DESIGN.md` as permission to replace a coherent incumbent identity or use app-interface rules for marketing work.

## Handoff

Record hierarchy, palette, type, density, depth, motion, state, and signature decisions. Delegate behavioral implementation to `implement` or `developing-changes` when available; otherwise use the direct-parent fallback.

## Completion

The direction is product-specific, explicitly accepted when material, traceable to evidence, and honest about unresolved decisions and unmet proof.
