# Colorize

Modified from Impeccable 4.1.1 at `56f44523f76efdcec813e67b38ee550e49b16f48` under Apache-2.0.

## Scope

Introduce or correct deliberate color roles in a monochromatic, flat, or semantically inconsistent interface.

## Diagnose

Map current primitives and semantic roles, theme behavior, natural product color world, focal action, statuses, data encodings, contrast failures, and unmotivated accents.

## Evidence

Show role assignment and contrast across light/dark themes, default/hover/focus/disabled states, color-vision-safe data distinctions, and representative screens.

## Guardrails

Do not decorate with color, multiply accents, hard-code literals beside tokens, encode meaning by color alone, or change established status semantics without approval.

## Handoff

Prefer semantic token correction over local utilities. Delegate implementation to `implement` or `developing-changes` when available.

## Completion

Color is scarce, product-specific, accessible, theme-consistent, and communicates action or meaning rather than filling space.
